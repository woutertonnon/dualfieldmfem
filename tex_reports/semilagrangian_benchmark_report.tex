\section*{Introduction}

This report collects benchmark results for the semi-Lagrangian
Navier--Stokes solver based on pullback advection of discrete 1-forms
(Tonnon \& Hiptmair, 2023, arXiv:2301.04923).

The velocity is discretized in $\mathrm{ND}_1$ (first-order N\'ed\'elec
elements, $H(\mathrm{curl})$-conforming) and the pressure in
$\mathrm{CG}_1$ ($H^1$-conforming).  Advection is handled by a
semi-Lagrangian pullback step: edge DOFs are updated by integrating the
old discrete 1-form along transported edges (characteristic tracing +
cell-splitting line integration).

The diffusion--pressure step solves the following saddle-point system at each
time step.  The bilinear form contains \emph{no Eulerian convection term}
because convection is accounted for by the semi-Lagrangian step:

\begin{align}
	a_h(u,v)
	         & := \frac{1}{\Delta t}(u,v)_\Omega
	+ \nu(\nabla\times u,\nabla\times v)_\Omega                                                     \\
	         & \quad + \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\nu\langle n\times(\nabla\times u),v\rangle_F
	+ \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\nu\theta\langle u,n\times(\nabla\times v)\rangle_F               \\
	         & \quad + \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\frac{\nu C_w}{h_F}\langle n\times u,n\times v\rangle_F,
	\\[0.2em]
	b(p,v)   & := (\nabla p,v)_\Omega,
	\qquad b^\top(u,q):=(u,\nabla q)_\Omega.
\end{align}

The right-hand side uses the advected field $\tilde\omega$ (output of the
semi-Lagrangian step) in place of $u^n$:
\[
	F(v) := \frac{1}{\Delta t}(\tilde\omega,v)_\Omega + (f,v)_\Omega
	+ \text{Nitsche boundary terms},
	\qquad
	G(q) := \langle u_D\cdot n,q\rangle_{\partial\Omega}.
\]

Since the system matrix is \emph{constant} across time steps (no convection
dependence), the UMFPack factorization is computed once and reused.

\section{TaylorGreenSemiLag}

Taylor--Green combined space-time convergence benchmark.  Because the
semi-Lagrangian advection is restricted to ND1, only order $o=1$ is tested.

\[
	\nu=0.01,\qquad k=1,\qquad T=0.125,\qquad
	\Delta t(r)=\frac{0.5}{8\,2^{r}}.
\]
\begin{align}
	u(x,t)
	       & =\begin{bmatrix}
		          e^{-0.02t}\sin(x_0)\cos(x_1)  \\
		          -e^{-0.02t}\cos(x_0)\sin(x_1) \\
		          0
	          \end{bmatrix},
	\qquad
	p(x,t)=0.
\end{align}

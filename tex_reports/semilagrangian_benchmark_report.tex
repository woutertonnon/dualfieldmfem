\section*{Introduction}

This report collects benchmark results for the semi-Lagrangian
Navier--Stokes solver based on pullback advection of discrete 1-forms
(Tonnon \& Hiptmair, 2023, arXiv:2301.04923).

Two variants are tested:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{First-order (ND$_1$/CG$_1$):} velocity in $\mathrm{ND}_1$
  (first-order N\'ed\'elec elements), pressure in $\mathrm{CG}_1$.
  BDF1 time stepping, Euler characteristic tracing.
\item \textbf{Second-order (ND$_2$/CG$_2$):} velocity in $\mathrm{ND}_2$
  (second-order N\'ed\'elec elements, Rapetti small-edge transport with
  $I_{h,2}$ projection), pressure in $\mathrm{CG}_2$.  BDF2 time stepping
  (BDF1 bootstrap), Heun characteristic tracing.
\end{itemize}

Advection is handled by a
semi-Lagrangian pullback step: edge DOFs are updated by integrating the
old discrete 1-form along transported edges (characteristic tracing +
cell-splitting line integration).

The diffusion--pressure step solves the following saddle-point system at each
time step.  The bilinear form contains \emph{no Eulerian convection term}
because convection is accounted for by the semi-Lagrangian step:

\begin{align}
	a_h(u,v)
	       & := \frac{1}{\Delta t}(u,v)_\Omega
	+ \nu(\nabla\times u,\nabla\times v)_\Omega                                                            \\
	       & \quad + \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\nu\langle n\times(\nabla\times u),v\rangle_F
	+ \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\nu\theta\langle u,n\times(\nabla\times v)\rangle_F                     \\
	       & \quad + \sum_{F\subset\partial\Omega}\frac{\nu C_w}{h_F}\langle n\times u,n\times v\rangle_F,
	\\[0.2em]
	b(p,v) & := (\nabla p,v)_\Omega,
	\qquad b^\top(u,q):=(u,\nabla q)_\Omega.
\end{align}

The right-hand side uses the advected field $\tilde\omega$ (output of the
semi-Lagrangian step) in place of $u^n$:
\[
	F(v) := \frac{1}{\Delta t}(\tilde\omega,v)_\Omega + (f,v)_\Omega
	+ \text{Nitsche boundary terms},
	\qquad
	G(q) := \langle u_D\cdot n,q\rangle_{\partial\Omega}.
\]

Since the system matrix is \emph{constant} across time steps (no convection
dependence), the UMFPack factorization is computed once and reused.

This solver is a single-field formulation: benchmark CSV files provide
$\|u_1\|$ and (when exact data are available) $u_1$ $L^2$ error.  Dual-field
diagnostics such as $\|u_2\|$, $u_1\cdot\omega_1$, and $u_2\cdot\omega_2$
are included in the same panel layout when available, and omitted otherwise.

\part*{Second-Order Semi-Lagrangian Results}

\section*{Second-Order Solver and Stabilization Parameters}

The second-order solver uses $\mathrm{ND}_2$ (second-order N\'ed\'elec
elements) and $\mathrm{CG}_2$ ($H^1$-conforming quadratic Lagrange) for the
velocity and pressure, respectively.  Advection is performed via Rapetti
small-edge transport: pullback integrals are evaluated on $d(d+1)/2$ small
edges per element, projected via the $I_{h,2}$ operator, and converted to
MFEM ND$_2$ DOFs.  Time stepping uses BDF2 (with a BDF1 bootstrap for the
first step).
